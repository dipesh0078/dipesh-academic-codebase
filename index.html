Comparative Analysis of LSTM, Transformer, and TCN in Predicting Peptide-MHC Class II Binding Affinity

Dipesh Thapa · 2025

Journal of Advanced College of Engineering and Management, 11(1), 1–13.

Comparative evaluation of LSTM, Transformer, and TCN architectures on 134,281 IEDB2016 peptide–MHC binding records across regression and classification tasks.

TCN achieved R² = 0.6208 and AUROC = 0.8766; five-fold cross-validation and ensemble modeling improved R² to 0.6805.
